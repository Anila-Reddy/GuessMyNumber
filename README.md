# Guess My Number! 🎯

A fun number-guessing game where you try to guess a random number between 1 and 20. Challenge yourself to get the highest score!

## 🎮 How to Play

1. The game generates a random number between **1** and **20**.
2. Start guessing the number!
3. After each guess, you will receive feedback:
   - **Too high!** if your guess is higher than the number.
   - **Too low!** if your guess is lower than the number.
   - **Correct!** if your guess matches the number.
4. Your score decreases by 1 for every incorrect guess.
5. Try to beat your **high score** by guessing correctly with the least attempts.

## 🏆 Features

- Real-time feedback on your guesses.
- Tracks your score and high score.
- Simple, interactive, and fun to play!
